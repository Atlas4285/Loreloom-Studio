### assistant-top

```text
Create one transparent decorative top layer for a long assistant reply in a themed chat interface, 4:1 landscape canvas. Concentrate small engraved mithril corner fittings, fine gold leather tooling, and restrained dark leather binding folds in the upper-left and upper-right edges, within narrow outer margins. A very faint trace of deep slate-blue dyed parchment may connect the decorations along the upper edge; the central reading area remains almost entirely transparent. Palette: midnight slate #0D1420, blue-gray #28394B, royal gold #FFD06A, cool mithril blue #A8E8F6, and a very small crimson #F27981 accent. Regal Western fantasy craftsmanship, strong dark-light contrast, fine material detail, and disciplined metallic highlights. The lower two-thirds must gradually fade to fully transparent pixels, with no hard bottom boundary. The opposite side margins should also taper softly into transparency. Match the companion bottom layer and the seamless deep slate-blue dyed parchment texture in color, material, and lighting. Bright highlights stay outside the central 80% so pale text can remain legible. One cohesive decoration layer with real alpha transparency, not an opaque scene and not a rectangular UI frame. No text, letters, numbers, icons, buttons, controls, people, or bodies. Do not draw a checkerboard or a solid backing color. Export high-resolution transparent PNG.
```
