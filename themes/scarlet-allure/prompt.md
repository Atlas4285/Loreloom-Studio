### assistant-top

```text
Create one transparent decorative top layer for a long assistant reply in a themed chat interface, 4:1 landscape canvas. Concentrate narrow bright-pink satin folds, delicate floral lace fragments, and tiny champagne-gold jewelry glints in the upper-left and upper-right edges, within narrow outer margins. A very faint trace of soft violet woven fabric may connect the decorations along the upper edge; the central reading area remains almost entirely transparent. Palette: violet velvet #1D142B, plum #32213F, vivid candy pink #FF54B8, luminous rose pink #FF8BD2, blush #FFB8E3, champagne gold #FFD59D, and lilac #C69AFF. Seductive, intimate, flirtatious romance game atmosphere, like perfume-lit evening silk and a private whispered invitation. Pink accents remain bright and saturated. The lower two-thirds must gradually fade to fully transparent pixels, with no hard bottom boundary. The opposite side margins should also taper softly into transparency. Match the companion bottom layer and the seamless soft violet woven fabric texture in color, material, and lighting. Bright highlights stay outside the central 80% so pale text can remain legible. One cohesive decoration layer with real alpha transparency, not an opaque scene and not a rectangular UI frame. No text, letters, numbers, icons, buttons, controls, people, or bodies. Do not draw a checkerboard or a solid backing color. Export high-resolution transparent PNG.
```
