### assistant-top

```text
Create one transparent decorative top layer for a long assistant reply in a themed chat interface, 4:1 landscape canvas. Concentrate fine faceted crystal shards, slender celestial arcs, and a few crisp prism glints in the upper-left and upper-right edges, within narrow outer margins. A very faint trace of deep blue frosted glass may connect the decorations along the upper edge; the central reading area remains almost entirely transparent. Palette: midnight blue #0B1024, indigo #161F3F, luminous violet #B78CFF, cyan #4BDFFF, periwinkle #9CA9FF, and sparse sakura pink #FF6DC4. Elegant Japanese fantasy game atmosphere, polished and luminous, with controlled crystalline depth. The lower two-thirds must gradually fade to fully transparent pixels, with no hard bottom boundary. The opposite side margins should also taper softly into transparency. Match the companion bottom layer and the seamless deep blue frosted glass texture in color, material, and lighting. Bright highlights stay outside the central 80% so pale text can remain legible. One cohesive decoration layer with real alpha transparency, not an opaque scene and not a rectangular UI frame. No text, letters, numbers, icons, buttons, controls, people, or bodies. Do not draw a checkerboard or a solid backing color. Export high-resolution transparent PNG.
```
